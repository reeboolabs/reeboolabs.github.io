Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026

Nanieh is a digital garage: a service book for your vehicles. Your vehicle records belong to you: they stay on your phone, and the optional backup goes only into your own Google Drive. The developer never receives, sees or stores any of it.

What Nanieh stores, and where

  • On your phone only: the vehicles, services, reminders, fill-ups, expenses, documents, tires and modifications you enter, plus any photos, receipts or PDFs you attach. They are kept in the app's private storage on your device.
  • No accounts, no servers: Nanieh has no sign-up and no server of its own. It does not use analytics, tracking or advertising, and it does not sell or share data with anyone.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you turn on Google Drive backup, you sign in with your own Google account and Nanieh saves a backup to your Google Drive, in a folder called “Nanieh Backups”: one backup file, replaced each time, and your photos and documents, each uploaded once.

  • Nanieh asks only for Google's drive.file permission. That lets it see and change only the files Nanieh itself created. It cannot see any of your other Drive files.
  • The backup goes straight from your phone to Google. It never passes through the developer or any other server.
  • Nanieh reads your Google account's email address only to show which account is connected. It stays on your phone.
  • Your Google sign-in is kept in your phone's secure storage. Sign out in Nanieh's Settings at any time, or remove Nanieh's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Nanieh's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from your Google account is used only to back up and restore your own Nanieh data, and is never transferred to anyone else, used for advertising, or read by people.

Phone permissions

  • Camera and photos: used only when you choose to add a vehicle photo, a receipt or a document.
  • Notifications: reminders such as a service due by date or a document about to expire. They are created on your phone; nothing is sent from a server.
  • Face ID, fingerprint or passcode: if you turn on the lock, your phone checks it. Nanieh never receives biometric data.

Deleting your data

  • Settings → Delete all data clears everything in the app. Deleting the app removes it from your phone.
  • To delete the backup, remove the “Nanieh Backups” folder from your Google Drive.

Children

Nanieh is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
