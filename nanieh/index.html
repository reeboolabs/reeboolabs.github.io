Nanieh

Your digital garage. By REEBOO Labs.

A service book for every vehicle you own: when the oil was last changed, what went in, and when the next service is due. Everything stays on your phone; the optional backup goes only to your own Google Drive.

What it does

  • Maintenance cards: each job shows its last change, what was used and when it's due next, by mileage or date.
  • Quick logging: a new service starts from your last one, so a repeat oil change takes a few taps.
  • Fuel and costs: real economy between full tanks, and every cost counted once, in its own currency.
  • Documents and tires: reminders before insurance or registration expires; tire positions, rotations and tread.
  • Backup: optional, to a “Nanieh Backups” folder in your own Google Drive.
