Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

REEBOO Labs makes game audio, visual effects and asset packs. This page explains what we do with the information you share with us.

This website

reeboolabs.com sets no cookies and runs no analytics, tracking or advertising. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Email and messages

When you email us or message us on a platform, we keep your name, address and the conversation so we can answer you and deliver the work you ask for. We use it for nothing else, never sell or share it, and delete it when you ask.

Our company mailbox is Google Workspace. Our internal mail tool connects only to our own mailbox through the Gmail API to read and send our business email. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Purchases and payments

Packs are sold through stores such as itch.io, and payments go through PayPal or the store. They handle your payment details under their own privacy policies; we receive only what we need for the order (for example your name, email and what you bought).

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, email Alrabia@reeboolabs.com. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
