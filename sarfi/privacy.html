Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

Sarfi is a personal expense tracker. Your money data belongs to you: it stays on your phone, and the optional backup goes only into your own Google Drive. The developer never receives, sees or stores any of it.

What Sarfi stores, and where

  • On your phone only: the expenses, income, savings goals, notes, categories, warranties and settings you enter, plus any bill photos or files you attach. They are kept in the app's private storage on your device.
  • No accounts, no servers: Sarfi has no sign-up and no server of its own. It does not use analytics, tracking or advertising, and it does not sell or share data with anyone.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you turn on Google Drive backup, you sign in with your own Google account and Sarfi saves a backup to your Google Drive, in a folder called “Sarfi Backups”: one backup file, replaced each time, and your bill photos.

  • Sarfi asks only for Google's drive.file permission. That lets it see and change only the files Sarfi itself created. It cannot see any of your other Drive files.
  • The backup goes straight from your phone to Google. It never passes through the developer or any other server.
  • Sarfi reads your Google account's email address only to show which account is connected. It stays on your phone.
  • Your Google sign-in is kept in your phone's secure Keychain. Sign out in Sarfi's Settings at any time, or remove Sarfi's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Sarfi's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from your Google account is used only to back up and restore your own Sarfi data, and is never transferred to anyone else, used for advertising, or read by people.

Phone permissions

  • Camera and photos: used only when you choose to attach a bill or receipt.
  • Notifications: reminders such as a bill due tomorrow, payday, or a warranty ending soon. They are created on your phone; nothing is sent from a server.
  • Face ID: if you turn on the lock, your phone checks your face or passcode. Sarfi never receives biometric data.

Deleting your data

  • Settings → Delete All Data clears everything in the app. Deleting the app removes it from your phone.
  • To delete the backup, remove the “Sarfi Backups” folder from your Google Drive.

Children

Sarfi is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
