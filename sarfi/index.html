Sarfi

A wallet-style expense tracker, in Omani Rials.

See your month as real banknotes in a wallet, log spending in a tap, and save toward goals. Everything stays on your phone; the optional backup goes only to your own Google Drive.

What it does

  • Monthly wallet: your salary and payday fill the wallet; spending takes notes out.
  • Savings goals: plan by date or monthly amount, auto-save on payday, and track what you spend from each goal.
  • Bills and warranties: keep receipt photos with expenses and get reminded before a warranty ends.
  • Search: find any expense by its note, category, goal or amount.
  • Backup: optional, to a “Sarfi Backups” folder in your own Google Drive.
