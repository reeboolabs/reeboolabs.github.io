32 heroes, 10 animations, 4 directions

Eight classes in four colours: knight, ranger, mage, rogue, cleric, barbarian, farmer and alchemist, on 16x32 frames.

Every hero has idle, walk, run, attack, cast, hurt, death, dash, interact and tool, facing down, left, right and up. Each one also comes as 5 aligned layer sheets, and 175 modular sheets (4 bodies, 48 hair, 36 headgear, 64 outfits, 20 items, 2 shields) let you mix your own.

The town around them: 24 NPCs that idle, walk, talk and work, and 12 animals that idle, walk, run, eat and sleep, in the same 4 directions.

48 enemies and 8 bosses

Eight families: beast, demon, elemental, goblin, insect, plant, slime and undead.

Every enemy has idle, walk, attack, hurt, death and special in 4 directions, and 2 colour variants, so 96 more to place. The bosses are 48x48 or 64x64 with 7 animations each. 15 projectiles cover the shots in between.

16 tilesets and a whole village to fill them

12 biomes and 4 interiors, with 7,222 unique tiles and 133 terrain transitions.

Meadow, farm, forest, dark forest, desert, snow, swamp, beach, volcanic, mountains, graveyard and autumn, plus house, castle, dungeon and cave. Cliffs, stairs, paths, walls, fences, bridges and doors are in every set, with animated water and lava. Each tileset comes as a Godot 4.4 TileSet .tres with 42 terrain sets, collision, navigation and custom data, as Tiled .tsx/.tmx, and as Unity slice JSON.

Around them: 449 props (34 animated, such as chests, torches, the campfire and banners), 72 trees in 24 kinds and 3 sizes, and 12 crops in 5 growth stages with 12 harvested items.

Menus, dialogue and fonts in place

The templates already have a title menu, dialogue with portraits, an inventory grid, a hotbar, a HUD with hearts and coins, pause and options menus, and saves. Two included packs dress them: Pixel UI Kit (742 sprites in 7 themes) and Pixel Font Family (8 fonts). The kit adds 48 portraits and 16 emote bubbles.

Add 12 stingers, 24 ambience loops and 183 SFX in 60 types. Everything comes as WAV 44.1 kHz 16-bit and OGG, loudness-matched. The 9 included audio packs add 2,896 more sounds, so the bundle holds 3,155 audio files.

Three Godot 4.4 games to start from

Small, complete games made only from the kit: title, menus, saves, HUD, enemies, bosses, dialogue, inventory and farming.

Action RPG

4 hero classes and 3 maps (village, forest, mine) with walk-in exits. 8 NPCs with portrait dialogue, a shop and an inn, 7 chests, and 7 enemy kinds with 3 behaviours: chaser, shooter and hopper. The Slime King boss has a second phase. Loot drops, a 20-slot inventory with weapon, armour and trinket slots, day and night with a music swap, a pause menu and save and load.

Farm & Cozy

A hotbar with a hoe, a watering can and 6 seed kinds. Till, water, plant, grow, harvest and sell. 12 wandering animals you can pet, a clock and day cycle, sleep to end the day, and save and load.

Dungeon Roguelike

Procedural floors with rooms that lock until cleared, a 12-enemy tiered pool and treasure rooms. A boss waits every third floor: the Lich, the Golem King and the Demon Lord. Auto-equip loot, and permadeath with a run summary.

One shared addon of 37 GDScript files runs all three. MegaMap builds maps from ASCII rows on any of the 16 tilesets, MegaSheet turns any kit sheet into SpriteFrames, and the player, enemy, boss, NPC, inventory, dialogue, day and night, save and audio nodes drop into your own project.

Questions

Which engines can I use it with?

The templates and the TileSet files are Godot 4.4. The art is plain PNG with JSON layouts, so any engine that loads sprite sheets can use it. Unity gets a slicer script and a how-to, Tiled gets .tsx/.tmx, and the audio README covers GameMaker. We tested the Godot path and the browser build. We did not test the files inside Unity, Tiled or GameMaker.

Which file formats are inside?

Art: hard-edged transparent PNG sheets, one strip per animation and direction, a layout JSON per sheet, Godot 4.4 TileSet .tres, Tiled .tsx/.tmx, and Unity slice JSON with an Editor slicer script (SliceFromJson.cs) plus a Unity how-to. Audio: WAV 44.1 kHz 16-bit and OGG. Templates: Godot 4.4 projects (Compatibility renderer, with keyboard, gamepad and touch overlay). You also get an offline HTML catalogue and one manifest with every file and count.

What does the license allow?

Royalty-free use in commercial and non-commercial games and apps. Modify the assets as you like; credit is appreciated but not required. You may not resell or redistribute the assets as they are, alone or in another pack. One LICENSE.txt covers the kit, and each included pack carries its own copy of the same terms.

Can I build a full game with it?

Yes. The three templates are small, complete games made only from the kit. Copy one, replace the maps, the item and dialogue JSON and the text, and build from there with the same addon.

Is 16x16 the only size?

Native tiles are 16x16, characters 16x32, and bosses 48x48 or 64x64. The art zip also carries 2x and 3x nearest-neighbour copies of every PNG.

What was tested, and what are the known limits?

We tested on Godot 4.4.1 (Windows): every template imports headless and passes its smoke test, and the browser build runs in Chrome. The templates README lists the known limits: enemies steer straight at the hero without navigation, there are no house interiors, and the roguelike boss arena is a plain room. Not tested: balance and real phones.
